      Applying

      Dry run does everything a real application does (opens the posting, fills every field, uploads your résumé and cover letter, answers the questions) and stops just before the final Submit. It's logged like a real application. Nothing reaches the employer.

      Your AI provider

      JobHunt uses the LLM you choose to summarize postings and tailor your documents. Bring your own API key. It is stored in MindAttic Vault on this computer, never in the JobHunt database or an export.

      Backup & restore

      Everything JobHunt knows in one .jobhunt-backup.json file: every applicant, their requirements, jobs, and applications with their history. Importing one replaces what's here. API keys and job-site passwords are never included.

      Deleted applicants

      Deleting an applicant hides them. Their data stays until you restore them.
